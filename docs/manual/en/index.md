# The Trama manual

Trama is a qualitative data analysis program. You bring in interviews, focus groups, field notes, documents and open survey answers; you mark passages and give them codes from a codebook you build as you go; you write memos; you describe your participants with attributes; and you look for patterns across the whole material. Everything stays on your computer, encrypted, and Trama never connects to the internet.

This manual is also inside the app: choose **Help** in the navigation, search it from the box above the contents, or press `Ctrl+K` and type the name of a chapter.

1. [Getting started](01-getting-started.md) — installing Trama, your library, the passphrase and the 12-word recovery phrase, projects, the example project, locking, settings
2. [Documents](02-documents.md) — importing text, Word, PDF and other files, transcripts and their recordings, survey answers, document groups and cases
3. [Coding](03-coding.md) — selecting and coding passages, the codes panel, in-vivo codes, keyboard coding, coding stripes, editing the text, memos on passages
4. [The codebook](04-codebook.md) — the code hierarchy, definitions, merging and splitting codes, exchanging codebooks
5. [Coded segments and memos](05-retrieval-and-memos.md) — retrieving what you coded, filters, copying and exporting segments, writing memos
6. [Cases and attributes](06-cases-and-attributes.md) — cases, attributes, importing attributes from a spreadsheet
7. [Analysis](07-analysis.md) — whose codings are counted, text search, co-occurrence, code × document matrices, code × attribute tables, word frequencies, charts
8. [Teamwork and inter-coder agreement](08-teamwork-and-agreement.md) — copies for teammates, merging, coders, agreement statistics and what they mean
9. [Exchanging projects](09-exchange.md) — REFI-QDA with NVivo, MAXQDA and ATLAS.ti, the `.trama` file, what carries over
10. [Privacy and security](10-privacy-and-security.md) — encryption, backups, pseudonymisation, the audit trail, text for ethics applications and data management plans
11. [Glossary](glossary.md)

The most useful [keyboard shortcuts](01-getting-started.md#shortcuts) are in the first chapter; Help also has a page listing all of them.

## Conventions in this manual

- **Bold** words are the names of buttons, menus and screens as you see them in Trama.
- Keys look like this: `Ctrl+Z`. On a Mac, use `⌘` (Command) wherever this manual says Ctrl; Help shows the keys the Mac way when it runs on a Mac.
- Almost every change can be undone with `Ctrl+Z` straight away, and redone with `Ctrl+Shift+Z`. Where something cannot be undone, Trama asks first and the manual says so.
- Screens and buttons may move as Trama develops. If something here does not match what you see, the command palette (`Ctrl+K`) finds any screen or action by name.
