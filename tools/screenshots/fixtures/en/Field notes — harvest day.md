# Field notes — harvest day

*Fictional material for the Trama manual. Everyone and everything here is invented.*

Saturday, Kiln Hill. Arrived at 9:40; overcast, dry. About forty people across the morning.

## The long table

Plot holders brought what they had too much of: marrows, beans, a crate of windfall apples. Nobody priced anything. Teresa kept a list of who took what "so we know what to grow more of", not to keep count of who owes whom.

## Newcomers

Two families who joined in spring stayed by the shed until Arthur asked them to help weigh the squash. After that they stayed until the end.
