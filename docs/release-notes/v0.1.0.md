## Trama 0.1.0

The first release of Trama: free, open-source qualitative data analysis that keeps every project encrypted on your own computer, with no account and no network connection.

**New in this version: record and transcribe, on your computer.** Record an interview held over Zoom, Teams, Meet or any other call app (your microphone and the other side, kept apart), and transcribe it, or any recording you import, with a speech model that comes inside the app (Whisper large-v3-turbo through whisper.cpp). Nothing is uploaded. The transcript arrives as speaker turns with time stamps that follow the recording. Recording asks you to confirm that everyone agreed to be recorded.

Also in Trama: import text, Word, OpenDocument, RTF, PDF, subtitles and transcripts, and survey answers; code with a hierarchical codebook; memos, cases and attributes; coded-segment retrieval, text search, co-occurrence, matrices and word frequencies; inter-coder agreement and merging a teammate's copy; pseudonymisation and an audit trail; encrypted automatic backups; REFI-QDA exchange with NVivo, MAXQDA and ATLAS.ti. In English, European Portuguese and European Spanish.

**Downloads**

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 and 11 | `Trama_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe` (recommended), or an `.msi` in English, Portuguese or Spanish |
| macOS 14.2 or later (Apple silicon and Intel) | `Trama_0.1.0_universal-adhoc.app.tar.gz`: unpack, move `Trama.app` to Applications |
| Linux | `.AppImage` (any distribution, Fedora and openSUSE included: make it executable and open it), `.deb` (Debian, Ubuntu) |

Each installer is about 600 MB: almost all of it is the speech model, so that transcription works without downloading anything.

**Before you start**

- These builds are **not code-signed**. Windows SmartScreen warns: choose *More info → Run anyway*. On macOS, open the app once, then *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway*.
- On macOS, the first recording asks for the microphone and for *System Audio Recording*: allow both. Recording the computer's sound needs macOS 14.6.
- Transcription needs a 64-bit processor with AVX2 (Intel and AMD since about 2013) or Apple silicon, and takes roughly half to the whole length of the recording.
- Trama is at an early stage. Keep copies of your data, and use automatic backups.
- **Trama (example)** (Windows Start menu, the menus after installing the `.deb`, or `--example`) opens a fictional study, passphrase `trama`, that is emptied every time: a safe place to try everything.

## Português

Em inglês, português europeu e espanhol europeu.

**Transferências**

| Sistema | Ficheiro |

**Antes de começar**

- O Trama está numa fase inicial. Guarde cópias dos seus dados e use as cópias de segurança automáticas.
